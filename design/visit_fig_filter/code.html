Finding Your Way

1
From the High Street

Turn down Weaver Street right past the stationers. Walk roughly sixty paces until you see the weathered brick archway on your left.

2
Through the Archway

Step through into the small cobblestone courtyard. We occupy the corner shop with the deep fig-purple bay windows.
